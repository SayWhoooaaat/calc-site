Rank Everything Always

An Android app for making tier lists of anything: chips, beer, TV shows, dog breeds. It has more than 100,000 downloads on Google Play.

Make a list, add items from the camera, the photo gallery, an image search or plain text, then drag them into ranked rows. Rows can be added, renamed and recoloured, and a finished list can be exported as an image and shared.

Lists are stored on the phone itself, so there is no account to sign up for.

I mostly use it to keep track of which dark beers I like.
